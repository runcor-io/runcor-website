## 🛡️ Is It Safe?

**Yes - Bank-level security.**

Every job runs in a **Docker container** - like a sealed box:

- ✅ **No access to your files** - Job only sees its own folder
- ✅ **No network access** - Can't connect to internet
- ✅ **Resource limits** - Uses max 4GB RAM, won't slow your PC
- ✅ **No admin privileges** - Can't install anything
- ✅ **Verified jobs only** - We check every job before it runs

Think of it like Airbnb for your computer - guests stay in a separate room, not your bedroom.
